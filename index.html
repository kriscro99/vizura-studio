Process

From brief to brand in five weeks.

A fixed, transparent sequence. You always know what happens next, and when.

  1. 01 · Week 1

    Discover

    A focused workshop on your market, competitors and ambitions. We agree on what the brand must say before anything is drawn.

  2. 02 · Weeks 1–2

    Define

    Positioning, personality and a sharp creative brief: the yardstick every design decision is measured against.

  3. 03 · Weeks 2–4

    Design

    Concepts presented in real context (product UI, pitch decks, signage), then refined through focused revision rounds.

  4. 04 · Week 5

    Deliver

    Master files, guidelines and a launch kit, organised so your team can use them from day one.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Ask directly: get in touch.

How long does a project take?

A logo takes around two weeks, a full brand identity four to five, and a company website four to six. The exact timeline is fixed in your proposal before we start.

How does payment work?

50% when we start, 50% on delivery. Larger projects can be split into milestones. All prices are in euros and exclude VAT.

Do we own the final design?

Yes. Once the final invoice is paid, full ownership and copyright of the final artwork transfer to your company.

What do you need from us to start?

A short brief about your company, audience and competitors, plus one decision-maker available for the workshop and reviews. Vizura guides you through the rest.

Can you redesign our existing logo?

Absolutely. Many clients come for a refresh: evolving an existing mark so it keeps its recognition but works on today’s screens and products.

Do you work with companies abroad?

Yes. Vizura works with B2B and tech companies worldwide, remotely, in English and across time zones.
